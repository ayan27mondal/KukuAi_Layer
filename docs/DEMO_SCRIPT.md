# A short walkthrough of AI Lawyer

This outline is for a three-minute project demo. The timings are approximate; adjust them to fit the pace of the presentation.

## 0:00-0:20 | Introduce the project

Say: "AI Lawyer is a prototype for people in India looking for general information about everyday legal problems. It can help organize the facts, find relevant notes, and explain possible next steps. It is not a lawyer and does not replace legal advice."

## 0:20-1:10 | Describe a problem by voice

In the sidebar, record a Hindi or Hinglish example such as: *"Mera landlord deposit wapas nahi kar raha, 40 hazaar rupaye hain. Main do mahine pehle Guwahati, Assam mein ghar khaali karke aaya."*

Show the transcript and answer any follow-up questions about the agreement or other missing details. Then point out the case summary, proof checklist, and plain-language response. Open **Sources used** to show where the answer's citations come from. If a state-coverage caveat appears, explain that the notes do not cover every state equally.

## 1:10-1:50 | Create a document draft

Enter sample details in **Your details**, then choose **Create draft**. Show the `[TO BE FILLED]` fields and remind viewers that they must check every detail before using the draft. Download the DOCX or PDF and briefly show the next-steps guidance.

## 1:50-2:20 | Try practice mode

Choose **Practice** and respond to the landlord's objection about painting charges. Try to state the amount, relevant dates, proof you have, and what you are asking for. Type `end` to finish and show the feedback.

## 2:20-2:50 | Show the safety limits and evaluation

Ask the app to guarantee a win or provide a court strategy. Show how it avoids promising an outcome. Then try a message about being threatened with arrest and show the escalation response. If time allows, open the latest evaluation report under `tests/outputs/` and mention both the metrics and any failed cases.

## 2:50-3:00 | Close

Say: "This is a prototype, so its answers and drafts need review. The goal is to make the available information easier to follow while being clear about when to contact a lawyer or legal-aid service."
