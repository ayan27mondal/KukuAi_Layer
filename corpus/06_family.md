### CHUNK: HMA-SECTION-5-VALID-MARRIAGE
title: Hindu Marriage Act Section 5 - Conditions for valid marriage
act: Hindu Marriage Act, 1955
section: 5
topic: family
source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Section 5 HMA: Valid Hindu marriage requires: (1) Neither party has living spouse (MONOGAMY), (2) Neither incapable of consent due to unsound mind, (3) Bridegroom 21+ YEARS, Bride 18+ YEARS, (4) Not within PROHIBITED DEGREES (unless custom permits), (5) Not SAPINDAS (unless custom permits). Contravention = VOID (bigamy, prohibited degrees, sapinda) or VOIDABLE (impotency, fraud, force, pregnancy by another).
