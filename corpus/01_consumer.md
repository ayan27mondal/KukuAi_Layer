### CHUNK: CPA2019-S2-CONSUMER
title: Who is a consumer, defect, deficiency definitions
act: Consumer Protection Act, 2019
section: 2(7), 2(10), 2(11)
topic: consumer
source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019: (1) "Consumer" (Section 2(7)) = person who buys goods or hires/avails services for consideration (paid/promised/partly paid/partly promised) for personal use, NOT for resale or commercial purpose. Self-employed persons using goods/services for livelihood ARE consumers. (2) "Defect" (Section 2(10)) = fault, imperfection, shortcoming in quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard of goods. (3) "Deficiency" (Section 2(11)) = fault, imperfection, shortcoming, inadequacy in quality, nature, manner of performance of service.

verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: CPA 2019 Chapter VI (Sections 82-87): Product liability claim can be filed against: (1) Product manufacturer, (2) Product service provider, (3) Product seller.
