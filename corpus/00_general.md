### CHUNK: GEN-COGNIZABLE-VS-NON
title: Cognizable vs Non-Cognizable Offences
act: BNSS 2023 / BNS 2023
section:
topic: general
source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: COGNIZABLE OFFENCE: Police CAN ARREST WITHOUT WARRANT, MUST register FIR, investigate on own. Examples: murder, rape, theft, robbery, dowry death, cheque bounce (NI Act 138), domestic violence breach. NON-COGNIZABLE OFFENCE: Police CANNOT arrest without warrant, CANNOT investigate without MAGISTRATE ORDER. Enter in daily diary, direct complainant to Magistrate. Examples: defamation, simple hurt, criminal trespass, most petty offences. BAILABLE: bail as RIGHT. NON-BAILABLE: bail at COURT DISCRETION. Check BNS Schedule for classification.
