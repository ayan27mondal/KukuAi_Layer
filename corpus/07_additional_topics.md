### CHUNK: NI-ACT-138-CHEQUE-BOUNCE
title: Negotiable Instruments Act Section 138 - Cheque bounce offence
act: Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
section: 138
topic: civil
source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Section 138 NI Act: CHEQUE RETURNED UNPAID for INSUFFICIENT FUNDS = CRIMINAL OFFENCE. CONDITIONS: (1) Cheque for LEGALLY ENFORCEABLE DEBT/LIABILITY, (2) Presented within 3 MONTHS/validity period, (3) Payee gives WRITTEN NOTICE within 30 DAYS of dishonour memo, (4) Drawer FAILS TO PAY within 15 DAYS of notice receipt. PUNISHMENT: 2 YEARS IMPRISONMENT AND/OR FINE up to 2x CHEQUE AMOUNT. COGNIZABLE, BAILABLE, COMPOUNDABLE. COMPLAINT within 1 MONTH of cause of action (expiry of 15-day notice period).

### CHUNK: BAIL-TYPES-BNSS
title: BNSS - Types of Bail (Regular, Anticipatory, Default)
act: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
section: 437, 438, 439, 187
topic: general
source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: BNSS BAIL TYPES: (1) REGULAR BAIL (Section 437/439): AFTER ARREST. Magistrate (Sec 437) / Sessions Court (Sec 439). Factors: gravity, evidence, flight risk, tampering. (2) ANTICIPATORY BAIL (Section 438): BEFORE ARREST - apply to Sessions/High Court fearing arrest for NON-BAILABLE offence. Conditions: cooperate, not leave India, not tamper. (3) DEFAULT BAIL (Section 187): If INVESTIGATION NOT COMPLETE in 60 DAYS (offence <10 years) / 90 DAYS (offence >=10 years). BAILABLE offences: RIGHT to bail. NON-BAILABLE: COURT DISCRETION.

### CHUNK: COGNIZABLE-VS-NONCOGNIZABLE
title: BNSS/BNS - Cognizable vs Non-Cognizable Offences
act: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
section:
topic: general
source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: COGNIZABLE OFFENCE: Police CAN ARREST WITHOUT WARRANT, MUST register FIR, investigate on own. BNS Schedule I examples: murder, rape, kidnapping, robbery, theft, dowry death (Sec 80), cruelty (Sec 85), cheque bounce (NI Act 138), domestic violence breach (PWDVA 31). NON-COGNIZABLE: Police CANNOT arrest without warrant, CANNOT investigate without MAGISTRATE ORDER. Enter in daily diary, direct to Magistrate. Examples: defamation, simple hurt, criminal trespass, petty offences. BAILABLE: bail as RIGHT. NON-BAILABLE: bail at COURT DISCRETION. Check BNS Schedule I for classification.
