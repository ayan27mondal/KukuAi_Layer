act: Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
section: 9
topic: civil
source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Section 9 CPC: Civil courts have jurisdiction to try ALL SUITS OF A CIVIL NATURE except those expressly or impliedly barred.
