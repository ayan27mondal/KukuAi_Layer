### CHUNK: CODEWAGES-S17-MONTHLY-WAGES

act: Code on Wages, 2019
section: 17(1)
topic: salary
source: https://labour.gov.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Section 17(1) Code on Wages 2019: Employer MUST pay monthly wages BEFORE EXPIRY OF 7TH DAY of following month. Daily-wage/weekly/fortnightly workers have shorter deadlines (daily: same day or next; weekly: within 2 days of week end; fortnightly: within 2 days of fortnight end). Appropriate Government (State/Centre) may notify different time limits - CHECK YOUR STATE RULES.

### CHUNK: BONUS-ACT-ELIGIBILITY

section: 2, 8
topic: salary
source: https://labour.gov.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Payment of Bonus Act 1965 STILL IN FORCE (not subsumed yet). Applies to establishments with 20+ employees. Employee eligible if: (1) Salary up to Rs 21,000/month (Section 2), (2) Worked 30+ days in accounting year. Minimum bonus: 8.33% of salary/wages. Maximum: 20%. Must be paid within 8 months of accounting year end. Check if your employer covered.

act: Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
section: 2, 4
topic: salary
source: https://labour.gov.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Gratuity Act 1972: Applies to establishments with 10+ employees. Employee eligible after 5 YEARS continuous service (4 years 240 days for seasonal). Gratuity = 15 days wages x years of service (max Rs 20 LAKH). Payable on: retirement, resignation (after 5 years), death, disablement. Employer must pay within 30 days. Nomination required.

section: 2(23), 2(81)
topic: salary
source: https://labour.gov.in/
verified: true
ai_checked: yes
text: Code on Wages 2019 definitions: "Employee" (Section 2(23)) excludes: (1) Apprentice, (2) Person in managerial/administrative capacity drawing > Rs 15,000/month (or notified amount), (3) Person in supervisory capacity drawing > Rs 18,000/month (or notified) exercising mainly supervisory functions. "Worker" (Section 2(81)) similar. High-salary managers may have different remedies - check contract and civil law.
